Interactive Border Gateway Protocol (BGP) Simulation

Configure eBGP between Autonomous Systems (AS 10 & AS 20), inspect BGP tables, and test route withdrawal.

○ BGP Peering: Idle / Down

STAGE 1 Autonomous Systems Topology Builder

Place 4 PCs, 2 Switches, and 2 Routers. Connect PC0/PC1 to Switch0 and Switch0 to Router0 using Straight-Through cables (AS 10). Connect PC2/PC3 to Switch1 and Switch1 to Router1 (AS 20). Connect Router0 to Router1 using a Copper Cross-Over cable.

STAGE 4 BGP Table & Path-Vector Prefix Inspector

Live real-time inspection of the BGP database (show ip bgp) across Autonomous Systems. Notice the AS-Path attribute prepended to learned customer prefixes.

STAGE 5 🧪 BGP Link Failure & Route Withdrawal Simulator

Test how BGP dynamically reacts when an inter-AS physical link drops. Toggling the link down breaks the TCP session, triggering authentic Cisco interface flap syslog messages and withdrawing the remote AS routes.

● Link State: UP (GigabitEthernet0/1 Active)

STAGE 6 Cross-Autonomous System Ping & Packet Simulation

Transmit ICMP Echo Requests from PC0 (192.168.10.2 in AS 10) across the eBGP WAN boundary to PC3 (192.168.20.3 in AS 20).
