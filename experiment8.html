Exercise 8 - A: Configuration of Single Area OSPF (Area 0)
Construct the 3-Router mesh topology → assign IP addressing → configure OSPF Area 0 on Routers 1, 2, and 3 → verify neighbor adjacencies and end-to-end ping.

STAGE 1 Physical Topology Construction (Part 8A)

Drag 2 PCs (PC0, PC1) and 3 Routers (Router1, Router2, Router3) onto the canvas.
Connect them with cables: PC0 → Router1 (Copper Crossover), Router1 → Router2 (Copper Crossover), Router2 → Router3 (Copper Crossover), Router1 ↔ Router3 (Serial DCE), and Router3 → PC1 (Copper Crossover).

Exercise 8 - B: Configuration of Multi-Area OSPF
Construct Star-of-Areas topology → assign IP addressing across Area 0, Area 1, and Area 2 → configure central ABR Router 0 and perimeter routers with OSPF Process 15 → verify inter-area routing.

STAGE 1 Physical Topology Construction (Part 8B)

Drag 6 PCs (PC0…PC5), 3 Switches (Switch0…Switch2), and 4 Routers (Router0…Router3) onto the canvas.
Connect PCs to Switches (Straight-Through), Switches to Routers (Straight-Through), and perimeter Routers 1, 2, 3 to central ABR Router 0 (Copper Crossover).
